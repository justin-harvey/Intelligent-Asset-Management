The honest status

Read this plainly

This is an early-stage idea with a working prototype (MVP). It is not funded, and it is not yet running in a real port. Everything in the data room is a researched plan, not proven product-market fit.

What comes next

The next step is not a fundraise, it's market outreach. We talk to EV-charging operators and port operators to confirm the need is real and to line up genuine interest. That traction, signed design partners and letters of intent, is what justifies and funds the real build and the custom development each customer requires.

We are not raising yet. When traction justifies it, early funding would go roughly: ~60% product and engineering (the detect→dispatch→verify engine plus the first cross-vendor adapters), ~25% design partners and sales, ~15% everything else. Figures are illustrative.
