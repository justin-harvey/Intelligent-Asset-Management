Pricing & Packaging

How we charge

Price the unit of value, the uptime of a port, as a premium layer on top of the CPMS. Land on reliability, expand by ports and asset classes.

Recommended model (a proposal to validate)

A per-active-port annual subscription, positioned as the O&M / compliance layer above the CPMS, tiered by volume, with an optional compliance-assurance tier. It meters on the exact unit of value (a port that must stay above 97%), it is simple for a buyer with a NEVI budget line to approve, and it grows automatically as the operator adds ports and asset classes.

This is a pricing hypothesis. M6 Tests 2 and 4 validate the budget line and willingness-to-pay before we lock it.

The unit of value

NEVI makes the port the unit that must perform (above 97% uptime, measured per port). The ROI model puts ~$4,000–5,000 per port per year of value on closing the reliability gap. So we price per active port: it tracks value, it is easy to meter, and it aligns our incentive with the operator's, keep more ports up.

Benchmark: what the layer below charges

The CPMS layer runs roughly $25 per port per month (~$300/yr) (ChargeLab published; the band is ~$10–50/port/mo). We sit above it as a premium reliability/compliance layer. Pricing a Compliance seat near ~$360/port/yr is a credible premium add-on, and against ~$4,000–5,000/port/yr of value it captures under 10% of the value it creates. That is the definition of easy to say yes to.

Land, then expand

Land One workflow (charger fault → verified restoration) on a slice of the operator's ports, tied to the NEVI number.
Expand 1 · more ports Roll from the pilot slice to the full network as the uptime lift is proven.
Expand 2 · more asset classes In a port estate, extend the same engine to cranes, rail, pavement, utilities, priced per asset/site.
Expand 3 · more sites / networks New depots, regions, or acquired networks inherit the layer.

Each vector lifts net revenue retention without new logos, which is what turns a modest per-port price into a compounding account.
