Multi-Vendor Maintenance Automation

A cross-vendor maintenance platform for EV charging networks and port terminals, structured for a 5-year strategic acquisition.

Bottom line up front

One product, two initial markets. We start with EV-charging networks because federal regulations (NEVI) mandate 97% uptime, making repair speed critical, while no current software manages cross-vendor maintenance effectively. We validate our automated repair workflows at a port terminal, giving us quick access to test heavy industrial assets.

Exit: Near-term acquisition by EV hardware and charging networks (ABB, Nayax, ChargePoint), or a longer-term exit to enterprise asset management software vendors (IFS, Hexagon, IBM).

Status: Initial research complete. Next step: Live validation with 5 charging network operators and 1 port terminal operator. This is an entry hypothesis with a target acquirer list, pending real-world validation.
