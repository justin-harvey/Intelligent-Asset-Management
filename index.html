Multi-Vendor Maintenance Platform

An automated maintenance platform for EV charging networks and port equipment, built for a 5-year strategic acquisition.

Bottom line up front

One product, two initial markets. We start with EV charging networks because federal law (NEVI's 97% uptime mandate) penalizes downtime, yet operators lack software to manage repairs across different hardware suppliers. We prove our automated cross-vendor dispatch at port terminals, where we have direct access to test on heavy industrial equipment.

Exit path: A near-term strategic acquisition by EV hardware and network providers (ABB, Nayax, ChargePoint), or a scaled acquisition by enterprise software consolidators (IFS, Hexagon, IBM).

Status: Desk research complete. Next step: Live testing with 5 charging network operators and 1 port terminal operator. This is a tested entry strategy with target acquirers, pending real-world validation.
