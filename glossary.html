Glossary

Terms used in this room

Plain-language definitions, for readers who don't live in EV charging and asset management.

TermMeans
CPOCharge Point Operator, the company that runs a network of EV chargers. Our buyer.
CPMSCharge Point Management System, software that runs a charging network (sessions, billing, roaming, remote status). The layer below us.
NEVINational Electric Vehicle Infrastructure program, the US federal funding program whose rules include the 97% uptime mandate. See the detail.
Uptime (97%)The share of time a charging port is online and actually dispensing power. NEVI requires >97% average annual uptime per port.
OCPPOpen Charge Point Protocol, the open standard chargers use to talk to management software. How we read signals across vendors.
EAMEnterprise Asset Management, software of record for an organization's physical assets (e.g. IBM Maximo, SAP). A likely acquirer category.
CMMSComputerized Maintenance Management System, work-order/maintenance software, often a component of EAM.
RaaSRobotics- or Reliability-as-a-Service, a model where a vendor owns the hardware or the labor and bundles maintenance. The capture risk we design around.
DCIMData Center Infrastructure Management, the mature software layer that defends the data-center vertical.
DCFCDC Fast Charger, high-power public charger; the costliest to have down.
Level 2The common 240V AC charger (home, workplace, destination); most public ports.
MTTRMean Time To Repair, the average time to fix a down asset. The lever we pull on uptime.
Toutage / TexcludedMinutes a port is down / minutes excused under NEVI, the two fields behind the uptime formula.
AGV / RTGAutomated Guided Vehicle / Rubber-Tyred Gantry crane, examples of instrumented port-terminal equipment.
TRUCONNECTKonecranes' OEM condition-monitoring product, covers Konecranes equipment only, which is why the cross-OEM seam is open.
DERDistributed Energy Resources, behind-the-meter/grid-edge assets; an expansion vertical.
ICPIdeal Customer Profile, the specific buyer we target first. See GTM.
ACVAnnual Contract Value, the yearly revenue per customer.
CACCustomer Acquisition Cost, the cost to win a customer.
NRRNet Revenue Retention, how account revenue grows (or shrinks) year over year; land-and-expand drives it above 100%.
SAM / SOMServiceable Available Market / Serviceable Obtainable Market, the realistically addressable and winnable slices of the market.
