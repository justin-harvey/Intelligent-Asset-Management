Outreach & Validation · M6

Prove it before you build it

The five falsifiable tests, who to run them with, and ready-to-send scripts. Run all five before writing production code.

The ask, in one line

Talk to 5 unaffiliated CPOs and 1 terminal operator. Confirm reliability/dispatch is a top-3, funded, incumbent-can't-solve problem, then build the one narrow workflow. If Test 1 fails, drop to the next survivor (ports-first, then energy/grid) rather than abandoning the project.

Who to talk to

  • 5 CPOs that are not affiliated with us and not closed networks, regional/independent networks, fleet-depot operators, retail/site-host operators running mixed hardware.
  • 1 terminal operator for the ports beachhead, the design-partner candidate (direct access).

Decision rule: commit to build only if Tests 1–4 pass for EV charging and Test 5 shows a viable path (even if the original adverse-data thesis dies).

Baselines to capture (for the later ROI claim)

In every call, get numbers on: time-to-detection · time-to-dispatch · mean-time-to-repair · human touches per incident · % uptime vs the 97% SLA · evidence completeness for compliance reporting. These become the before/after in the Y2 "it moves the uptime number" proof.

Where to source five unaffiliated CPOs

  • NEVI awardee lists: state DOT NEVI program pages publish awarded operators; the long tail (not Tesla/EVgo) is exactly the target.
  • Fleet-depot operators: transit agencies, school-bus and municipal fleets electrifying depots; reliability is existential for them.
  • Retail/site-host networks: grocery, travel-center and hospitality operators running mixed hardware across sites.
  • Industry rooms: SEPA, EV charging reliability working groups, and reliability-focused threads (Paren, EVChargerScout) surface operators publicly complaining about uptime.
  • LinkedIn title search: "Head of Reliability / VP Operations / Director of Network Operations" at CPOs outside the big three.

Outreach scripts

1 · CPO cold email (validation interview, not a pitch)

Subject: 30 min on charger uptime, comparing notes, not selling

Hi [First name],

I'm researching how CPOs are actually hitting the NEVI 97% uptime
bar, specifically the maintenance-dispatch side when a unit goes
down across mixed hardware.

I'm not selling anything. I'm talking to a handful of operators to
pressure-test what's genuinely broken vs. what the CMS vendors claim
they've solved. In exchange I'll share what I'm hearing across the
other operators (anonymized); it's turned out to be useful to the
folks I've spoken with.

Would 25-30 minutes in the next week or two be worth it? Happy to
work around your schedule.

[Your name]
[one-line credibility: who you are / why this]

Why it works: leads with their problem and the funded mandate, explicitly disclaims a pitch (raises reply rate), and offers a cross-operator insight trade as the incentive.

2 · LinkedIn opener (shorter)

Hi [First name], researching how independent CPOs are hitting NEVI
97% uptime, mostly the dispatch-and-verify side across mixed hardware.
Not selling; trading notes with a few operators. Worth 25 min?

3 · Terminal operator: design-partner ask

Subject: Design partner, cross-OEM maintenance coordination at [Terminal]

Hi [First name],

Quick one. Terminals run cranes, rail, pavement and utilities from
different OEMs, and the single-vendor monitoring tools (TRUCONNECT
and the like) only see their own equipment. The coordination across
all of it (detect, dispatch the right crew, verify it's actually
fixed) still falls on your team.

We're building the layer that coordinates that across vendors and
asset classes, and we're looking for one terminal to build it with
as a design partner; your real problems shape the product, you get
it first, no cost during the build.

Could we grab 30 minutes to see if the fit is there?

[Your name]

Why it works: names the exact cross-OEM/cross-asset gap (the break-point finding), frames a design partnership (free during build, first access) rather than a sale, and asks for a single low-commitment call.
