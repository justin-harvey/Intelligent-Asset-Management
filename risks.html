An honest list. Each risk carries a likelihood/impact read and a concrete mitigation. Several of these also point at the eventual exit.

The one that would actually stop us

If forced to name a single kill condition: a dominant CPMS makes vendor-neutral, cross-estate coordination unnecessary, either by consolidating the market so "mixed hardware across vendors" stops being common, or by opening a genuinely neutral cross-CPMS layer themselves. We watch for both. Today the market is fragmenting (more operators, more hardware, more CPMS vendors), which cuts the other way, and no CPMS has any incentive to coordinate a rival's chargers or a port's cranes. That asymmetry is the moat.
